\url{https://doi.org/10.0000/example}

Then ask what you want them to take from it:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item A question about what the paper claims.
    \item A question that builds on their answer to the first.
    \item A question asking them to judge where the idea applies.
\end{enumerate}
