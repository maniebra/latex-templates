\begin{neobox}[neotitlefont=\pixelfont]{Rules, Notes and Policies}

    \vspace{5mm}

    \begin{center}
        \Large{Read the following before starting the assignment:}
    \end{center}

    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Review the course slides at least once before you begin.

        \item Using AI tools for research and search is fine. Handing the
              writing of your final answers to one is not, and any question
              found to be written that way is graded zero.

        \item Discussing problems with your classmates is encouraged. Sharing
              answers is not, and is handled under the university's academic
              integrity rules for both parties involved.

        \item The assignment has three parts: theoretical (35\%), research
              (25\%), and practical (40\%). It is worth \hwgrade{} point(s)
              of the course grade, scored out of 100 for precision.

        \item You are encouraged to draw on books, papers, and open-source
              projects\footnote{Cite whatever you use at the end of your
              answers.} to answer as precisely as you can.

        \item The late policy is the one agreed at the start of the semester.

        \item What you upload must be a single \texttt{.zip} archive named and
              laid out as follows:

              \begin{neocode}[neocodetitlefont=\pixelfont]{file\_format.txt}
\coursecode @-HW@\hwnumber@-[STDID].zip
|
|--\coursecode @-HW@\hwnumber@-[STDID].pdf
|--HW@\hwnumber@-Practical/
|       |
|       |-- *
              \end{neocode}

              where \texttt{STDID} is your student number. The PDF holds your
              theoretical answers together with the practical report; the
              practical files go in \texttt{HW\hwnumber\_Practical}.

        \item Unless a question says otherwise, you may use any language or
              toolchain. Python is suggested for speed. Whatever you pick,
              your report must briefly explain every package, library, and
              framework you used, and why.
    \end{enumerate}

    \vspace{1cm}

    Good luck.

\end{neobox}
