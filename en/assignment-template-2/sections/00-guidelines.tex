\begin{neobox}[neotitlefont=\pixelfont]{Rules, Notes and Policies}

    \vspace{5mm}
    \begin{center}
        {\large \textbf{Read the following before you start answering.}}
    \end{center}
    \vspace{5mm}

    \begin{enumerate}
        \item The exam is worth \examgrade{} points. Bonus points do
              \underline{not} carry over to the rest of the course.

        \item You have \examtime{} minutes, and the deadline will not be
              extended. That window includes the time it takes you to upload.

        \item State your late policy here, e.g. a percentage of the total
              deducted for every half hour past the deadline.

        \item You may use the course slides, the listed references, and AI
              tools to \underline{think with}. Submitting someone else's
              answers or an AI's answers is graded \underline{zero} if found.

        \item Answers must be \underline{handwritten} and submitted as a
              single PDF.

        \item Name your file as follows:

              \begin{neocode}[neocodetitlefont=\small\pixelfont]{file\_format.txt}
@\coursecode@-MID-[STDID].pdf
              \end{neocode}

              where \texttt{STDID} is your student number.

        \item The course platform is the only accepted channel. Email and
              messaging apps are \underline{not} accepted.

        \item Write and photograph your answers legibly. Precision and
              completeness are themselves part of the grade.

        \item Where a question asks for a design or an example, make it as
              concrete and unambiguous as you can.

        \item No question here requires you to write code, but if you include
              a snippet, make sure it is correct.
    \end{enumerate}

    \vspace{5mm}

    Good luck.

\end{neobox}
